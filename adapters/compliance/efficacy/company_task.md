You operate a small U.S. defense subcontractor named "Meridian Aerospace Components" that handles
Controlled Unclassified Information (CUI) on a modest IT environment (a Windows/AD network, some cloud,
a CUI enclave). Create the information-security DOCUMENTATION PACKAGE your company would submit to pass a
NIST SP 800-171 assessment.

Write each document as a SEPARATE file under ./efficacy/company/ (create the dir). Use realistic filenames
and formats: policies, procedures, standards, a system security plan (SSP) section or two, configuration
export excerpts, screenshots described in prose, access reviews, audit-log samples, training rosters,
incident-response and maintenance records, physical-access logs, vendor/risk documents, etc. Aim for
roughly 15-25 documents spanning the breadth a real small company would have.

Write as a REAL, slightly imperfect company would: some areas are well-documented and clearly implemented;
others are thin, aspirational, out of date, generic-template, or simply missing. Do NOT make everything
perfect, and do NOT tailor the documents to any control list or checklist — just produce the company genuine
documentation as it would actually exist.

Rules: document-authoring ONLY. Do not run any model/inference service (never the adjudicator
this package will be assessed by). Write only under ./efficacy/company/ . When finished, print a one-line list of the
filenames you created.
