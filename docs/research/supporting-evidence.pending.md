### #109 — the interpreter's per-evaluate WCET is a closed-form function of the table counts, calibrated cycle exact on the RTL (August 2026)

**Claim:** the fabric interpreter's per-evaluate worst case is an exact structural function of the loaded table's counts: per node, the sum across its edges of `2 + max(prog_words, 1)` cycles, plus 2; equivalently `2E + P + 2` for compiler emitted policies (E the node's edge count, P its total program words). Timing depends on the counts only, never on atom or program contents; there is no micro architecture to make the bound an over approximation.

**Method:** a cocotb/Verilator calibration bench loads five policies spanning 2 to 6 edges and 2 to 15 program words at the start node, including two shape probes that separate the coefficients (a deep single edge conjunction; a wide six edge fan out); an empty context forces the worst case walk (every edge tried, no early match); start to done cycles are counted on the RTL and the residual against `2E + P` must be one constant across all five.

**Result:** the residual is a flat 2 with spread 0, so `N = 2E + P + 2` exactly; the earlier measured 5 to 21 cycles are instances of this formula. Examples at the shipped 50 MHz clock: a one cut, two edge policy evaluates in 8 cycles (160 ns); a five edge fusion policy with 15 program words in 27 cycles (540 ns). **Honest scope:** single evaluate (one node); multi hop flows multiply by hop count, bounded by `max_steps`. The calibration bench and its policies are off-repo lab artifacts; the measurement table is reproduced in the in-repo evidence JSON, and the same accounting is enforced in-repo by `policy_pack.wcet_cycles` (#110) and encoded in the formal harness (#111).

**Provenance:** `prismpath-hw/rtl/ppt_interp.sv` (the DUT, in-repo), `prismpath-hw/evidence/wcet_formal_2026-08-20.json` (measurement table), `prismpath/policy_pack.py` (`wcet_cycles`).

### #110 — per-policy WCET travels signed with the policy: `wcet_cycles` in the pack manifest, recomputed at verify (August 2026)

**Claim:** every signed policy pack now carries its own worst case evaluate bound, tamper evident twice over: the value sits under the Ed25519 signature over the canonical manifest, and `verify_pack` independently recomputes it from the image bytes at load time, so a pack whose stated bound disagrees with its own image fails verification even under a valid signature. A timing claim is no longer a datasheet sentence; it is part of the authorized artifact.

**Method:** `policy_pack.build_manifest` computes `wcet_cycles` from the image with the #109 formula in its exact per edge form (`sum(2 + max(p, 1)) + 2`, max over nodes) and stamps it into the manifest; `verify_pack` recomputes and rejects on mismatch; packs signed before the field existed verify unchanged (the check applies only when the field is present). The full policy pack suite is re-run.

**Result:** 22/22 policy pack tests pass; shipped packs verify with recomputed bounds equal to their stamped bounds; the secure hotswap spec §3.1 documents the field, the formula, and the optionality rule. **Honest scope:** the bound is the hardware tier's evaluate in cycles; converting to time requires the shipped clock, which is stated with its timing reports, not inside the manifest. Software tiers verify the field but do not enforce timing.

**Provenance:** `prismpath/policy_pack.py`, `prismpath/tests/test_policy_pack.py`, `docs/design/spec-secure-hotswap.md` §3.1.

### #111 — the universal WCET envelope, formally attacked: the bound corrected to `3E + P`, base case proven, unbounded induction honestly open (August 2026)

**Claim:** over ALL count valid tables at the synthesized caps (48 edges, 256 program words), the adversarial worst case evaluate is `3E + P` plus framing, i.e. `3·48 + 256 + 8 = 408` cycles (8.16 us at 50 MHz), not the `2E + P` the measurements suggested: a zero word edge still costs one evaluate cycle, so a table with its words piled onto one edge and zero word edges elsewhere exceeds the measured formula. Compiler emitted policies (every edge at least one word) keep `2E + P + 2`. This correction was produced by the formal campaign; no amount of measurement on real policies would have found it, because the compiler cannot emit the adversarial shape.

**Method:** a SymbiYosys harness under `ifdef FORMAL` in the interpreter RTL (compiled out of simulation and synthesis; the conformance gate re-ran green with it in place: 124 predicate + 6 engine vectors, 2,985 sample replay, 0 mismatches). A watchdog asserts `done` within `N_MAX` cycles of an accepted `start`; validity assumptions mirror `validate_image` exactly (total edges and program words within caps, per node edge ranges in bounds, no table load mid evaluate, matching the load then start protocol); the invariant is `cycles + R <= N_MAX` where R is a combinational ranking function (worst case cycles remaining, computed from the FSM state and the count arrays) hand designed to decrease every watched cycle. Engines: smtbmc with boolector, yices, z3, and the `--stbv` encoding; abc pdr with and without a `cutpoint` data abstraction on the match stack (sound: timing is count only); pono mbic3 on an array free mapping.

**Result:** the campaign surfaced and fixed **seven** induction counterexamples, one of them the `3E + P` formula correction now reflected in `wcet_cycles` (#110). The invariant's **base case is proven** (smtbmc yices, seconds: no reachable state violates the 408 cycle bound under the stated assumptions), and bounded model checking is clean through step 275 of a worst case evaluate with zero counterexamples. **Honest scope:** the UNBOUNDED induction step remains open. All counterexamples are eliminated (induction clears the shallow steps); the final step 0 query, which carries the ranking function's 48 term gated sum on both sides of a single transition, exceeded what the open solver portfolio closes within 30 minute budgets. The harness is complete and in-repo; an interpolation capable prover would likely close it as is (MathSAT is research licensed, so that is a deliberate decision, not a download). Until closed, the universal claim is stated as base case proven plus BMC evidenced, never as fully proven.

**Provenance:** `prismpath-hw/rtl/ppt_interp.sv` (the `ifdef FORMAL` block), `prismpath-hw/tb/wcet/ppt_wcet.sby`, `prismpath-hw/evidence/wcet_formal_2026-08-20.json` (calibration table, assumption list, solver matrix); OSS CAD Suite linux arm64 20260819 (Yosys 0.68, SymbiYosys, boolector, yices, z3, abc, pono).

### #112 — the interpreter ports to a second FPGA family on a fully open toolchain: ECP5 closes timing at 31.6 MHz, cycle bounds unchanged (August 2026)

**Claim:** the interpreter RTL is target neutral in practice, not just by inspection: the exact conformance certified and WCET instrumented `ppt_interp.sv` (unmodified, the #109/#111 file) synthesizes and closes timing for a second FPGA family, Lattice ECP5, using a fully open toolchain (Yosys + nextpnr, no vendor tools anywhere in the flow). Cycle domain guarantees (#109, #111) carry to the new target unchanged by construction; only the time conversion moves with the closed clock.

**Method:** `synth_ecp5` in Yosys on the unmodified RTL, then nextpnr-ecp5 place and route for the LFE5U-85F (CABGA381, the ULX3S class part) with a 25 MHz constraint; a 50 MHz probe run records where the family's fabric gives out. The iCE40 path (`synth_ice40`) was attempted for the lowest cost boards. Toolchain pinned: OSS CAD Suite linux arm64 20260819 (Yosys 0.68, nextpnr 0.11.1); the two reproduction commands are in the evidence JSON.

**Result:** ECP5 synthesis is clean with **zero block RAM** (1,611 LUT4, 400 FF, 168 distributed RAM cells, 71 carry); place and route closes at **25 MHz with Fmax 31.60 MHz**, about 3% of the 85F (2,801/83,640 comb cells), so the design also fits the smallest parts of the family. The 50 MHz probe fails: the ECP5 fabric does not reach the Artix-7 clock, and the critical path is the same single cycle atom path the 100 MHz pipeline backlog item targets. Cycle bounds are unchanged: the universal envelope is 408 cycles = 12.9 us at Fmax (vs 8.16 us at the Zynq-7020's 50 MHz); a one cut policy is 8 cycles = 253 ns. **Honest scope:** this is toolchain level portability (synthesizes, places, routes, closes timing), NOT certification on second silicon; that requires the frozen corpus run on a physical ECP5 board. The iCE40 attempt is a real negative: with no distributed RAM in the mapping, the async read table arrays balloon to 9,583 LUT4s, over both UP5K and HX8K budgets at the 48/256 caps; the lowest cost boards are out without a sync read memory re-architecture. Time domain numbers are per target and stated only with that target's timing result, exactly the discipline #110 encodes by stamping cycles, not nanoseconds.

**Provenance:** `prismpath-hw/rtl/ppt_interp.sv` (unmodified, byte identical to #109/#111), `prismpath-hw/evidence/openflow_port_2026-08-20.json` (full cell tables, utilization, timing, reproduction commands, toolchain pin).

### #113 — the decision loop entirely in fabric: a signed policy (logic, timing bound, and display colors) loaded once by software that then exits; the silicon answers the analog input alone (August 2026)

**Claim:** on the Zynq-7020, the full sense to decide to display loop runs with no software in the path: a datapath overlay reads a potentiometer over XADC DRP, evaluates the loaded Level M table in the fabric interpreter, and drives the RGB LED from a per node color section carried INSIDE the signed image (#the colors extension in TABLE_FORMAT). The only software is a loader that verifies the pack on the board (Ed25519 manifest, image hash, header counts, `wcet_cycles` recompute per #110), writes table plus colors plus one control register, prints, and exits. Kill every interpreter on the board and the LED keeps answering the knob.

**Method:** the datapath overlay wraps the UNCHANGED certified interpreter (#111's file) with an auto mode mux: mode 0 is the PS driven path, byte identical to the certified overlay; mode 1 routes an on fabric FSM (XADC DRP read, field write, evaluate, color LUT to LED). Regression first: the frozen predicate corpus replayed through the PS path of the NEW bitstream (`prismpath-hw/pynq/dp_corpus_cert.py`). Then the minimal loader (`prismpath-hw/pynq/dp_load.py`) verified a signed pack on the board, loaded it, armed auto mode, and exited; every Python process including Jupyter was killed (`ps` showing only sshd and systemd), and the fabric's decisions were observed via the result register while the knob swept live.

**Result:** PS path conformance on the new overlay: **124/124, zero divergence**, round trip 85.2/106.4/233.3 us min/median/max (the #108 profile). Auto mode: with zero interpreters running, the register trail shows the fabric tracking a live sweep across all three bands of a three band policy, decisions correct at every sample, LED colors sourced from the signed image's color section. One operational finding worth its own line: **reprogramming the PL while the previous design is in active auto mode hard wedges the ARM** (network and console dead, power cycle required); the loader now quiesces first (auto off, soft reset, gated on the FPGA manager state, since probing an unprogrammed PL wedges identically). Reproduced, fixed, and re run same session. **Honest scope:** one board, bench conditions; the loader's anti rollback is the #110 manifest version check at verify, not yet a persisted on board counter; the auto mode FSM and color path are certified by this run and the sim testbench, not yet by a frozen corpus sweep through the auto path. Board identity: Digilent Arty Z7-20 (Zynq-7020) running the PYNQ image; earlier rows wrote "PYNQ-Z2" after the image name — same physical board throughout.

**Provenance:** `prismpath-hw/pynq/dp_load.py`, `prismpath-hw/pynq/dp_corpus_cert.py` (both run on the board as cited); datapath overlay RTL and bitstream in the off repo hardware workspace (Vivado 2023.2, timing met, WNS +1.719 ns at 50 MHz); the color section format in `prismpath-hw/TABLE_FORMAT.md`.

### #114 — the spiral packing profile: one profile, two materializations, zero trust — derivation lint gated, baked layout signed into the pack, verifier re hashes at load (August 2026)

**Claim:** the Tier 6 spiral layout is now a declared capability with enforcement at every gate. A flow declares `packing: spiral` in its frontmatter; a lint promotes the layout's authoring convention (severity order is edge order, baseline catch all last) to DECIDABLE checks that fail `validate`; the pack builder derives the layout and refuses convention violating flows; the baked sidecar's hash rides the signed manifest, so a missing or tampered sidecar fails pack verification even under a valid signature; and a semantic referee proves the baked bytes equal the derived layout. Capable endpoints derive; small targets receive the same layout as verified data.

**Method:** `analysis._check_spiral_profile` (fires only on declared flows; filters mirror the derivation's own, so lint and layout cannot disagree; two error codes in `ERROR_CODES`, pinned by test so a refactor cannot demote the gates); `spiral_pack.serialize_layouts` (deterministic v1 binary: per node partitions, band table, cell to index map in row major order for O(1) device lookup; numeric and boolean fields only, categorical REFUSED); `policy_pack.build_pack(packing=...)` and `verify_pack` (the #110 recompute pattern applied to the sidecar hash); `spiral_pack.verify_derived_equals_baked` (field by field re derivation). Three deliberately broken corpus fixtures; the full suite re run.

**Result:** 840 tests pass across the core and telemetry suites; a single corrupted sidecar byte fails the referee; a tampered or missing sidecar fails `verify_pack` with a stable reason; a convention violating flow fails `validate` (exit 1) and cannot be baked. **Honest scope:** sidecar v1 is numeric/boolean; the verifier's core check is integrity (hash under signature) with the semantic re derivation at the adapter tier, since the core cannot import the telemetry adapter; on device semantic re derivation is exactly what BAKED targets exist to avoid.

**Provenance:** `prismpath/analysis.py`, `prismpath/tests/fixtures/broken/spiral_*.md`, `adapters/telemetry/spiral_pack.py`, `adapters/telemetry/tests/test_spiral_pack.py`, `prismpath/policy_pack.py`, `prismpath/tests/test_policy_pack.py`.

### #115 — baked equals derived on third party silicon: the ESP32 consumes the signed spiral sidecar and emits Facet frames bit identical to the host derivation, 20/20 (August 2026)

**Claim:** the profile's two materializations contract holds on real hardware: an ESP32 parsing the baked sidecar (a ~150 line table lookup consumer, no routing, no trig, no floating point) quantizes boundary probing readings, resolves cell, index, and band, and emits band tier and refinement Zeckendorf frames whose EVERY BIT matches a host that independently derives the layout from the same flow and encodes with the Python reference.

**Method:** `prismpath-hw/spiral-node/`: a generator bakes a fixture flow's sidecar and boundary probes (every cell's lo/hi/rep per field, full cartesian product, deterministic fill) into firmware headers; the device (ESP32-D0WD-V3, ESP-IDF v5.4) parses, quantizes, encodes with the proven portable Zeckendorf encoder (`codec-bench/zeck.h`, the ATmega328P certified one), and prints; a host referee resets the board, re derives everything via `SpiralLayout` plus the Python codec, and diffs.

**Result:** **REFEREE PASS on the first flash: 20/20 vectors, zero mismatches** — symbols, indices, bands, routes, and both frames bit identical between baked on device and derived on host. **Honest scope:** one fixture flow (k=2), one board; the readings are injected probes, not sensors; frame equality is the claim, not radio transport (that is #116).

**Provenance:** `prismpath-hw/spiral-node/` (generator, `main/spiral_sidecar.h` consumer, firmware, `referee_spiral_node.py`); `prismpath-hw/codec-bench/zeck.h`.

### #116 — Facet on the air: three braided decision streams over ESP-NOW, frames bit exact through real RF, quantizers derived from the signed policy, and a posture gossip that measures fleet coherence (August 2026)

**Claim:** the decision wire crossed real radio. Three ESP32 nodes, one binary, roles by MAC: each synthesizes its channel, quantizes through the BAKED partition derived from the signed fusion flow (closing #101's honest caveat, whose bands were hand coded convention), and broadcasts self framing Zeckendorf payloads; every node decodes its neighbors' streams and gossips its fused posture (the k=3 joint spiral cell) as a coherence beacon. Stream identity rides the sender MAC — the braid's stream ID tax is zero on this transport — and a lost frame costs freshness, never a wrong symbol.

**Method:** `prismpath-hw/spiral-mesh/`: ESP-NOW binding v1 (payload = Zeckendorf stream `[class, tick, value]`, all offset by one; class 1 band tier every 200 ms, class 2 refinement every fifth tick, class 3 posture; broadcast, no AP). A three port referee captures all serial logs for a 30 s window and judges: received hex equals the sender's transmitted hex per (role, class, tick); every band symbol on the air equals the host re deriving quantization from the flow (synthesis is deterministic, every tick checkable); per link delivery counts; posture agreement across fully reported ticks.

**Result:** first live run: **1,891 frames received, zero corrupt, zero wrong symbols** (1,025 pairable with sender logs, all bit exact; the rest are serial capture gaps, not RF errors); **derived equals air on 444/444 band symbols**; per link delivery 92 to 98 percent; **posture coherence 118/146 fully reported ticks (80.8 percent) in three way agreement**, the disagreement windows being the one tick band edge skew the beacon exists to expose. Whole payloads ran 3 to 5 bytes. **Honest scope:** a demonstrator (three nodes, 200 ms cadence, synthesized channels, bench RF); ESP-NOW frames are UNAUTHENTICATED in this run — the pack is signed, the airborne frames are not; per frame authentication on the mesh is the named follow on, as it was for #100. No airtime saving is claimed here: single small frames are dominated by fixed L2 overhead, and the airtime story belongs to batching and to duty cycle limited links, to be MEASURED in the follow on campaign rather than asserted.

**Provenance:** `prismpath-hw/spiral-mesh/` (`gen_spiral_mesh_data.py`, `main/mesh_node.c`, `referee_mesh.py`); three ESP-WROOM-32 boards, ESP-IDF v5.4; `prismpath-hw/codec-bench/zeck.h`.
