### #121 — the decision-delta object runs live across three machines: signed value + state + time, send-on-delta, decision-exact on the FPGA LEDs (August 2026)

**Claim:** the three-axis decision object runs live, end to end, across three separate physical machines, and its TIME axis is the **signed** receipt (the `bpf_ktime_get_ns()` stamped inside the tamper-evident, policy-bound record of #119), not a side log. A dev station emits raw control events; the certified eBPF `posture_selector` on a second machine DECIDES (value axis: signed Level M predicates) while holding a RESIDENT posture (state axis: the in-kernel FSM of #119); only posture *changes* are forwarded to the FPGA (time axis: send-on-delta), each carrying the receipt's `seq` + signed `t_ns`; the FPGA renders the posture on its LEDs. Holds transmit nothing — the LED's hold-time is the time-delta made physical. It is decision-exact: every delta the selector commits is the posture the LEDs show, in order, carrying the same signed `t_ns`.

**Method:** three machines. (1) **gx10** (aarch64 dev station) sends control events over UDP (`escalate`=1, `de-escalate`=2, `hold`=0), knowing nothing of postures. (2) A **Protectli** (x86_64, Linux 6.x) runs `sel_forward_receipts`, which loads and runs the CERTIFIED `ppt_select` eBPF program (the #119 selector, evaluator byte identical) via `BPF_PROG_TEST_RUN`, holds the resident posture in `sel_state_map`, and after each event **drains the receipt ringbuf** (`ring_buffer__poll`) to capture the kernel-emitted `struct ppt_receipt {seq, t_ns=bpf_ktime_get_ns() at commit, policy_hash, prev_node, event, next_node}` — then forwards SEND-ON-DELTA (only when the posture changed) to the board, carrying `next seq t_ns`. (3) The **Arty Z7-20** (Zynq-7020, PYNQ) runs `led_listen.py`, which loads the `ppt_pathb` overlay and writes each received posture to the RGB LEDs LD4/LD5 (`normal`=green `0x12`, `elevated`=amber `0x1b`, `lockdown`=red `0x09`), logging the upstream `seq`+`t_ns` beside its local receive time. Wedge-safe bring-up: the board's resident auto-mode design (`ppt_datapath_zeck`, the `prismpath-demo` flight recorder) was disarmed via its **hwh-derived** ppt-IP register — not a hardcoded probe — before the overlay load (`quiesce_zeck.py`). The scenario is the 9-event walk: escalate, hold, escalate, hold, hold, de-escalate, de-escalate, escalate, escalate. Both trails are frozen and SHA256-summed.

**Result:** **9 events -> 6 deltas forwarded + 6 physical LED renders, 3 holds suppressed (0 transmitted).** The board rendered exactly the selector's six committed transitions, in order (normal->elevated->lockdown->elevated->normal->elevated->lockdown), and **every board render's upstream `t_ns` matches the forwarder's receipt `t_ns` byte for byte** — decision-exact across the whole gap. The receipt trail carries a **monotonic signed `t_ns`** (`bpf_ktime_get_ns()` at commit) and binds to the loaded policy via `policy_hash 207748442a7915c8`; the session **Merkle root over the receipts is `624fb50d31df357f14a6a85775fa69192da6fcc0d182c5dac2a7d82b7ca3842a`** — the per-session anchor object of #119, now produced from a live forward path driving physical hardware. Holds cost zero transmission: the three `hold` events advanced no posture (`prev == next`) and sent nothing, so the LED (and the wire) moved only when the decision moved. (A first attempt rendered only 4 of 6 because the board's listen window elapsed during operator latency before the drive; re-run with a longer window, all 6 landed — a timing artifact, not a logic defect.) **Honest scope:** one bench, one session, one selector policy (the posture ladder). This is a live DEPLOYMENT of the #119-certified selector on a forward path, **not a new conformance certification** — the evaluator is byte identical, so #119's in-kernel 624/624 carries; the selector runs via `BPF_PROG_TEST_RUN` in a userspace forward loop, not attached to a NIC. The FPGA here **renders** the decided posture — it does not decide; the eBPF selector on the Protectli is the sole decision authority (the board is the display, the "third machine" of the object's reach, not a second decider). The **SPACE axis** (correlated-field spiral packing) is **NOT in this pipeline**; it is proven separately (#113-#116 fabric + mesh, #120 real-RF interference soak). The single pipeline that unifies all three axes including spiral space is the named walker-mesh follow-on, not this row. The signed `t_ns` makes the full receipt per-session (the decisions remain the deterministic oracle, reproducing every run); the OTS anchor of the Merkle root is the held-for-publish step (owner-gated).

**Provenance:** `prismpath-ebpf/decision-delta-demo/` — `sel_forward_receipts.c` (the receipt-draining forwarder), `led_listen.py` (board render endpoint), `drive_scenario.py` (dev-station driver), `quiesce_zeck.py` (hwh-derived wedge-safe quiesce), `RUNBOOK.md`, and frozen `evidence/{receipt_trail.log, led_render.log, forwarder_session.out, decision_delta.SHA256SUMS}`; the selector program `prismpath-ebpf/ppt_select.bpf.c` + `ppt_common.h` (the signed `t_ns` receipt of #119). Three machines: gx10 (aarch64), Protectli warden-node-01 (x86_64, Linux), Arty Z7-20 (Zynq-7020, PYNQ). Companion to #119 (the certified resident selector) and to the space-axis rows #113-#116 / #120.
